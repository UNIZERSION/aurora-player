Montserrat-Medium.ttf is the original source used by LVGL 9.3.0 for its
built-in lv_font_montserrat_* fonts (weight Medium, not Regular or Bold).

Upstream pinned release:
https://github.com/lvgl/lvgl/blob/v9.3.0/scripts/built_in_font/Montserrat-Medium.ttf

The generated UI fallback fonts contain U+00A0..U+017F at 14, 16, 18, 20,
24 and 32 pixels, with four-bit antialiasing and no runtime decompression.
Regenerate with ../generate_latin_fonts.py and lv_font_conv 1.5.3:
https://github.com/lvgl/lv_font_conv

ui_fonts.cpp copies the original LVGL descriptors and links the matching
Latin fallback without changing the library's immutable font objects.
ASCII glyphs, ASCII-pair kerning, and FontAwesome glyphs are unchanged.
Kerning is available within each font subset; cross-subset pairs do not kern.
Vertical line metrics include all accents and ogoneks. LVGL label heights
must use the returned font's line_height (19/22/24/26/31/41 pixels).
The bitmap fonts live in the firmware; no SD font files or dynamic TrueType
rasterizer are needed. The original TTF is only a reproducible build input.
